# CK Makam

Make music with darbuka and bağlama in your browser. Pick a makam (Hicaz, Rast, Uşşak, Kürdi, Hüzzam, Nihavend), pick an usul rhythm (Maqsum, Çiftetelli, Karşılama 9/8 and more), record a loop and download it as MP3, WAV or separate stems. Works on phones, tablets and desktops. Free, no account, no server: everything runs in your browser.

Live: https://cemilkocan.github.io/ck-makam/
